Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information AnimeStream handles when you browse the site, create an account, or save your library.

Effective date: November 8, 2026

1. Information we handle

Account information. If you create an account, AnimeStream processes your email address and password through its server-side account service, which stores the email address and a salted, one-way password hash in its Supabase database. The original password is not stored in readable form. The account service also creates a revocable session token so your account can remain signed in on that device. Account creation does not currently send an email confirmation or provide automated password-reset emails.

Your library and preferences. The site can store information you choose to save, including My List, Continue Watching entries, playback progress, manga reading progress, language and playback preferences. When you are signed in, these items are synchronised to your account so they can be available on another device.

Device storage. When you browse as a guest, site data such as saved items, preferences, recent searches, and temporary catalogue or preview caches may be kept in your browser's local storage. Temporary advertising data may also be kept in session storage. Clearing your browser's site data may remove information stored only on that device.

2. How information is used

  • To create accounts, verify passwords against salted password hashes, and maintain revocable account sessions.
  • To save and synchronise your library, reading and viewing progress, and preferences.
  • To remember settings and improve the basic operation and reliability of the site.
  • To request and display sponsored advertisements and measure ad delivery, where advertising is enabled.
  • To protect the service against misuse and troubleshoot technical problems.

3. Service providers and third parties

AnimeStream uses Supabase database and Edge Function infrastructure to operate its custom account service and store account libraries. The account service, rather than Supabase Auth, checks credentials and manages sessions. The site also requests catalogue information, images, video previews, manga data, and/or media playback from third-party providers. When you open an external link or embedded player, that provider may receive information such as your IP address, browser details, and the request you make.

The site also requests sponsored video advertisements from an external advertising provider. The advertising provider and tracking endpoints associated with an ad may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser or device information, ad impressions, and clicks. Those providers may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy notices and settings.

Third-party providers may change over time. AnimeStream does not control the privacy practices, availability, or content of services operated by other organisations.

4. Storage, security, and retention

Guest data is stored in the browser on the device you use. For account holders, account records, password hashes, session-token hashes, and library data are stored in separate database tables. Browser clients cannot directly read or write these private tables; account operations are handled by a server-side function that checks the session token and scopes library operations to the matching account.

We retain account and synchronised library information while it is needed to provide the account and sync features, unless it is deleted earlier. Signing out removes the active session from that device but does not delete the account or its cloud library. Individual saved titles and progress entries can be removed from the account page. Browser-only information can be removed by clearing the site's data in your browser. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, but reasonable technical controls are used to protect the service and its data.

5. Your choices

  • You can browse without signing in; guest information stays on the device where it was created.
  • You can sign out from the account menu or choose not to create an account.
  • You can remove individual titles and progress entries from your account page. Full account deletion is not currently self-service.
  • You can clear browser site data to remove local information stored on that device.
  • You can choose whether to open third-party links and embedded media. Those services apply their own privacy policies.

The current site does not provide a self-service full account deletion control or automated password recovery. Keep your password safe. If you lose access to it, contact the AnimeStream operator through the official contact method published for the service. A monitored privacy/support contact address should be published by the operator alongside this policy.

6. Children

AnimeStream is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children who are below the minimum age required to use the service in their location. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information should contact the service operator through its published contact channel.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the site or its providers change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a revised policy is posted. Continued use of the service after an update means the revised policy applies to future use.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the AnimeStream operator using the official contact details published for the service. The operator should add a monitored privacy or support email address here before public launch.
